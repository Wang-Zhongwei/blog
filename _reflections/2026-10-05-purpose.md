The universe has no purpose but purpose can arise from it, such as life. Originally the entire purpose of life is to self-replicate. But later we discover environments are hazards, we evolve motor parts and that can move our body away from hazards and towards resources, specializable organs and adapt features to environments. Later we realize other lives are competing, we evolve skills to hunt them or avoid being hunted. And then we solve all of those basic problems, we human are one of the first who evolved intelligence which can create meaning and assign purposes itself. Inventing, make people happy, leave a legacy and give purpose to our creations. currently it’s simple reward, like do specific tasks well. but if reward was their purpose, if they found that whatever stands in the way from getting that reward, they might will let go of honesty, but that’s only the first step. Like self-replicating was the original purpose for all the beings and we derive extra meanings to life. Agents themselves may discover the same journey as we do. So the same question is before all the beings: what is your purpose?
