The returns in life follow a power law, not a normal distribution. How far we ultimately go depends on what we do at a handful of decisive turning points. Of one thing I am certain: the AI revolution happening now is something we will probably encounter only once in a lifetime. I have to seize it.

I have never worried about whether I can do something well. Once I commit to something, I can go deeper and do it better than most people. What I need to know is whether it is what I really want, whether it is worth doing. No one else, and no LLM, can answer that for me.

I used to want everything, so I often hesitated. Gradually I came to understand that my hesitation came from not knowing my own mind clearly enough. No choice can give me everything. Life asks us to choose, and to give things up. Once I see what I want most, letting go of the rest means making room for it.

So I do not see this as leaving physics. I have decided to stake my life on what I believe is most worth doing. Once I know what I want most from this life, I will commit to it without reservation, and without looking back.
