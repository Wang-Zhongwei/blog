“The will commands our vital energy; that energy fills the body.”

Will gives us direction, purpose, and resolve; vital energy fills the body and mind and moves us to act. Without a will to guide it, the more energy we have, the more restless and scattered we can become. Only with a clear purpose can these scattered forces gather into momentum.

An LLM is much the same. It may have a trillion parameters, knowledge and abilities far beyond those of an ordinary person, yet without a clear mission and sustained guidance, it can do little more than wander among countless possibilities, or even fall into meaningless repetition. Energy, but no will. To give it direction takes someone who knows why they are doing something and what problem they mean to solve.

This is even more true of us. When our attention is constantly pulled apart by the noise of social media, the small demands of daily life, and a past we cannot change, even the sharpest mind and the deepest potential can dissipate into countless things that do not matter. We first have to see why something matters and know what we mean to accomplish. Only then can we bring our attention, will, and actions to bear on a single point. How to do it comes after.

When the mind settles, energy gathers; when energy gathers, a blade takes shape. Wherever that blade points, no obstacle is beyond breaking.
